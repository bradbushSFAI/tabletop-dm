greywater: A councillor lies dead in Greywater's long-empty Judge's House, the vote for his seat is ten days off, and both great families are already shouting murder.
tollhills: Two Tollhills families go to law over a prize ram named Magistrate, with a wedding riding on the verdict: can you keep the peace, the pies and a straight face?
brannocks-cut: In Brannock's Cut, the silver is thinning, the pit props are rotten and the miners want the sealed Ninth opened: what would you trade to keep three hundred families fed?
hushwood: The elves' yearly envoy is three weeks late, and tonight her pony walked out of the Hushwood alone, with her grey cloak tied carefully over its eyes.
sallows: Deep in the Sallows, a stilt village is pulling in its plank walks nine nights early, and an empty punt drifts home with its lamp still lit.
