greywater: In Greywater, a councillor lies dead in a house that has stood empty for twenty-two years, and the council votes in ten days: who profits, and who is lying?
tollhills: Two Tollhills families go to law over a prize ram named Magistrate, with a wedding riding on the verdict: can you keep the peace, the pies and a straight face?
brannocks-cut: In Brannock's Cut, the silver is thinning, the pit props are rotten and the miners want the sealed Ninth opened: what would you trade to keep three hundred families fed?
hushwood: At the Hushwood's edge, the elves' yearly envoy is three weeks late and her pony has walked out of the trees alone: how far in will you go?
sallows: In the Sallows, a stilt village pulls in its plank walks nine nights early, and a lit lamp drifts home in an empty punt: what do the fen folk know?
